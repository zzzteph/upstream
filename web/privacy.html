Upstream - Privacy Policy

The Naked Cat · Effective 28 November 2026

Upstream is a puzzle game made by The Naked Cat. This policy explains what information is used when you play it, in the Android app (Google Play) and in the web version.

What the game itself keeps

Upstream has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or any other personal details. It stores only your best score and whether you've seen the first-game walkthrough, on your own device. The developer doesn't receive this information.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional ad you can choose to watch for a second chance after a leak, and now and then a full-screen ad between games. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. If you don't consent, Google may still show non-personalized ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).
  • How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The web version

The web version has no ads and no analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when the page is loaded, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Upstream is not directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, contact the developer at the email address shown on Upstream's Google Play store listing.
